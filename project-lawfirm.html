PROJECT · PENETRATION TESTING LAB

Dakhel Law Firm Pentest Lab

A simulated internal network assessment of a law firm, hosted in Microsoft Azure. The attack begins by compromising a single external workstation, then pivoting into the law firm's internal network through a tunnel to simulate a realistic external-to-internal attack and report..

01 / OVERVIEW

What this project will cover

The scenario

Dakhel Law Firm is a fictional small law office with three employees: a lawyer, an IT support staff member, and a customer service representative. The lab will simulate a Windows 10 workstation connected over a VPN, mimicking an internal attacker's view of the network.

The goal is to demonstrate the full 5-phase penetration testing methodology — from reconnaissance to privilege escalation — with structured documentation and a professional report.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Planned attack chain

The lab will follow the standard 5-phase penetration testing methodology.

⌕

Phase 1 — Reconnaissance

Network discovery and port scanning to identify exposed services on the target endpoint. All findings will be documented with open ports and service versions.

◎

Phase 2 — Enumeration

SMB share enumeration and investigation of discovered services. The lab will include accessible shares with intentional misconfigurations.

⚡

Phase 3 — Initial Access

Obtaining low-privileged credentials from an exposed share, then using RDP to gain an interactive session on the target workstation.

◈

Phase 4 — Post-Exploitation

Local enumeration to identify privilege escalation vectors. The lab will feature a misconfigured service with writable binary path.

▲

Phase 5 — Privilege Escalation

Replacing the vulnerable service binary with a payload and restarting the service to achieve NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM — the highest privilege level on Windows.

03 / PLANNED FINDINGS

Vulnerabilities to be tested

Weak Credentials on Exposed Share

Critical

A shared folder with exposed plaintext credentials will be configured and tested for access control weaknesses.

Weak Service Permissions

High

A service running as SYSTEM with a writable binary path will demonstrate privilege escalation from a standard user.

RDP Enabled for Standard Users

Medium

Remote Desktop will be enabled without additional restrictions, increasing the potential attack surface.
