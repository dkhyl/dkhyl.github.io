METHODOLOGY

How I Work

A structured approach to understanding systems, identifying potential weaknesses, and documenting findings.

I approach security tasks methodically, starting with reconnaissance and gathering information before examining potential vulnerabilities. I document my process, validate findings in controlled environments where possible, and focus on clear evidence, accurate risk assessment, and practical recommendations.

Phases I follow

Each phase serves a purpose. A structured approach helps reduce missed details and supports reliable findings.

The depth of each phase depends on the scope and complexity of the assessment.

⌕

Reconnaissance

Gather initial information about the target and identify exposed systems and services.

◎

Enumeration

Examine discovered services to gather more detailed information and identify potential weaknesses.

✓

Validation

Verify potential findings using appropriate methods in authorized environments and assess their potential impact.

▤

Reporting

Document evidence, explain the risks, and recommend practical remediation steps.
