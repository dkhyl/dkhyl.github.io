BACKGROUND

My Journey

I graduated in Law from Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University in 2025, but my interest in cybersecurity goes back to my teenage years. My curiosity about technology grew when I read one of my brother's books about Cisco networking, which sparked my interest in networks and gradually deepened my passion for cybersecurity. For a long time, I saw penetration testing as a hobby without realizing it could become a career. Today, I'm turning that long-standing interest into a professional path through security training, hands-on labs, and practical projects.

BEYOND WORK

Beyond Cybersecurity

Outside of cybersecurity, I have a background in wrestling, boxing, and weightlifting, and I currently practice Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and MMA. These disciplines continuously challenge me physically and mentally, reinforcing deep resilience and adaptability.

Grappling shifted my perspective on failure: when I step onto the mat in my Gi, my primary goal isn't just to win, but to learn. I believe defeat begins in the mind long before a submission happens on the mat. Every setback highlights an area for growth, pushing me to return stronger and better prepared.

Striking taught me strategy and precision: sometimes, one well-timed punch is enough to disrupt an opponent's rhythm, recognize their habits, and find weaknesses to exploit. These lessons directly shape how I approach cybersecurity: staying calm under pressure, disrupting attack patterns, treating failure as feedback, and persisting through complex challenges until a solution is found.

Beyond these disciplines, I find peace in walking through nature, away from the noise of cities, traffic, and the rush of everyday life.
I enjoy quiet moments of reflection, looking up at the stars and reflecting on the hugeness of the universe, its planets, and the mysteries that lie beyond our great planet. These moments remind me how much there is to explore, understand, and learn. For me, growth comes not only from pushing my limits, but also from stepping back, appreciating the beauty of the universe, and reflecting on the mysteries that make it so fascinating.
